CLIENT PORTAL PAYMENTS + BDC PAYMENT INFO — what changed
=======================================================

1) BUG FIX — Client Portal showed the wrong total (e.g. Rs. 75,000 instead of Rs. 25,000)
   - Cause: the client's Payments page, Overview "Payment Progress" and "Next Payment" were
     built from the service's DEFAULT stage list (Registration + Start Work 15k + Code 25k +
     Paper 10k ...) and ignored the Total amount and split-up entered for that client.
   - Now they use the client's real plan (same logic the staff dashboards already use):
       Registration + the split-up installments entered for that client
       (+ any stage payment Accounts recorded), against the agreed Total amount.
       Any part of the total not split yet shows as one "Remaining balance" row.
   - Example: Total 25,000, Registration 25,000 paid  ->  Total Contract Rs. 25,000,
     Paid Rs. 25,000 (100%), Pending Rs. 0 "No dues — paid in full", one row: Registration.
   - "No service breakdown added yet." is no longer printed under every row; a breakdown
     only appears where one was actually added.
   - Old clients with no Total amount and no split-up keep the old stage-based schedule.
     So if a client still shows the default stages, their Total amount was never saved —
     enter it in the client profile.
   - The AI chat assistant no longer lists default stages (Start Work, Code, Paper) as
     "pending" for clients who have a Total amount / split-up.

2) BDC — client payment information now shown
   - Every BDC client row (My leads, Already in the pipeline, Registered, Technical,
     Accounts, pending lists) now shows:
       Total | Paid | Pending | x/y paid (or "Paid in full")
       + every split: Registration, each installment (paid / pending), and any balance
       "Not yet split".
   - Individual BDC staff (BDC Login) can now open the "Payment Information" tab on their
     own leads (it was hidden because they were treated like programmers/writers). They can
     mark Registration / installments as paid and add installments there — the server
     already allowed this on their own leads.
   - The BDC client drawer's Payments section now starts with the Total / Paid / Pending
     summary.
   - Leads that are not the BDC's own (sent without payment data by the server) show no
     money, same as before.
   - Fixed a crash risk: the BDC "Registered" and "Payment pending" lists read payments
     on clients that the server sends without payment data.

Files changed (1 + 2): index.html, ai_assistant.py.

3) Marketing TL / Marketing Manager no longer see other teams' employees
   - The "All Employees" board at the bottom of their dashboard is now "My Team — BDC"
     and lists only BDC staff (Programmers, Paper Writers and Journal teams removed).
   - Server: these two logins now receive full details only for BDC staff. Other teams
     come through as name + role only (needed for picking who gives a client demo) -
     no email, phone, date of birth, employee ID, branch etc.
   - Opening another team's employee profile from those logins is blocked.
   Files: index.html, server.py

4) Work-assigned emails to employees no longer include client contact details
   - Emails to Technical team members (programmers, paper writers, coordinators) and
     Journal team members (proofreaders, formatters, coordinators, submission) no longer
     contain the client's Phone, Email or overall Project deadline. They still get the
     client name/ID, service, current stage, topic, domain, and (for tasks) the task's
     own start and due dates.
   - Covers all three automatic emails: "new work assigned to you", stage hand-off
     "action needed", and "task assigned to you".
   - BDC staff still receive the phone and email (they need them to call the client), and
     department logins (Marketing, Accounts, Technical Manager...) are unchanged.
   File: server.py

5) "Work sent to you for review" email - client name, work number, attached document
   - The email to the Coordinator / Technical TL / Technical Manager now shows:
       Task    : Proposal writing
       Client  : Santhosh (CL-1001)
       Work    : Work 2 of 2 — IEEE Paper     (same numbering as the work badges in the app)
       Document: Proposal_Santhosh.pdf (attached)
     and the client name is in the subject line.
   - The proposal / paper the employee attached when sending the work is now attached to
     the email itself (it was only saved in the app before).
   - Email sending can now carry attachments (Gmail API uses Google's upload endpoint for
     these; SMTP works as before). Anything over ~18 MB in total isn't attached - the email
     says to open it from the dashboard instead.
   File: server.py

6) Sidebar scrolls + white strip at the bottom fixed (all dashboards, desktop)
   - Long menus (e.g. Technical Manager) now scroll inside the dark sidebar. Before, the
     bottom items (Dark mode / Log out) spilled out below the sidebar onto a white
     background and were cut off.
   File: index.html

7) "Code delivered" / "Paper delivered" / "Progress note" popup - one-tap options
   - The popup now lists ready answers to just select, e.g. "Demo completed — client
     satisfied", "Demo completed over a call — client happy with progress", "Demo completed
     — client asked for small changes", "Demo completed — client not satisfied, rework
     needed". Tapping one fills the note (tap again to clear); the note can still be edited
     or typed freely.
   - To change the list, edit WORK_UPDATE_QUICK_NOTES in index.html.
   File: index.html

8) Journal Manager / TL can assign Proofreaders, Formatters and Submission people directly
   - "New work needing assignment" -> Assign to: the list now shows the Proofreaders (or
     Formatters) themselves - tick one or more - plus the coordinators as an optional
     extra (max one).
       * Team members picked  -> work starts straight away (Proofreading / Formatting in
                                 progress) and they get the "new work assigned" email.
       * Only a coordinator   -> old flow: the coordinator picks the team.
       * Both                 -> team starts now, the coordinator still reviews.
   - A proofreader assigned without a coordinator now has "Approve & send to Journal
     Manager" / "Send correction to writer" on their dashboard, and re-checks the writer's
     corrected paper. A formatter assigned without a coordinator has "Approve & forward"
     (goes to the Journal Manager's final review) / "Request correction".
   - Formatting final review: new "Assign submission to" choice (default: whole Submission
     team). The chosen person gets the paper, the email and the reminder; other Submission
     members see it under "Assigned to other team members".
   - Pop-up reminders and emails follow the person actually assigned.
   Files: index.html, server.py (no database changes - existing columns are used)

9) Team list "Ongoing Project" count fixed
   - It used to count every client a person had ever worked on until the WHOLE project
     reached "Completed" - so e.g. a proofreading coordinator still showed 1 after
     proofreading was approved and the paper moved on to formatting.
   - Now a project is "ongoing" for someone only while their own part is open:
       proposal writer    - proposal being written
       programmers        - implementation in progress
       paper writers      - writing / fixing corrections
       content coordinator- paper review
       proofreading coord - coordinator assigned / proofreading / re-check
       proofreaders       - proofreading / re-check
       formatting coord   - formatting assigned / in progress
       formatters         - formatting in progress
       submission person  - waiting to be submitted
     plus a scheduled demo, or any unfinished task on that client assigned to them.
   - Same rule in the Export (CSV) and in the member profile's "x completed" figure.
   File: index.html

10) Dashboard boxes now use the Technical Manager box design everywhere
   - Journal Manager / Journal TL dashboard (Publications, Pending Actions, Journals),
     every Journal team employee dashboard (proofreaders, formatters, coordinators,
     submission) and every Technical team employee dashboard (programmers, paper writers)
     now draw their stat boxes exactly like the Technical Manager's: title, big "X / Y",
     the two labels, a progress bar and a short note.
   - The "My Proofreading / My Formatting / Demo Pending ..." panels use the same header.
   - "Correction Assignment" box no longer stretches to the calendar's height.
   - Journal Manager "Journals" box could show a negative number (e.g. "0 / -1") - fixed:
     it now shows Still pending / In journal pipeline.
   File: index.html
